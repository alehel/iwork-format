# Licence of the fixtures

The sample documents in the `fixtures` release were made for this project's experiments, by Apple's Pages, Keynote and Numbers driven by the recipes in `recipes/`.

What the project contributed to them is released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): the text typed into them, the images, sound and movie the recipes generated, and the test theme's cards and layout changes.

They also contain material that came with the apps and isn't the project's to license: the built-in styles, layouts and placeholder text of the blank templates and of Basic White, on which the test theme is built. No images from Apple's themes or templates are included; `fixtures/build.py` checks this for every file.
