# Keynote

What is specific to Keynote documents. The container is the same as Pages' ([container.md](container.md)), text is stored as in [2. Text](text.md) and styled as in [3. Styles](styles.md), and charts, sounds and movies are described in [5. Drawables, media and charts](drawables.md). Every finding links to the experiments that support it, with a confidence level as defined in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md#confidence).

Observed with Keynote 15.4 on macOS 26.5.2.

## Parts

A new presentation has the seven `.iwa` parts that Pages documents have, without numbers in their names, plus one `Index/Slide-N.iwa` per slide and one `Index/TemplateSlide-N.iwa` per template slide of the theme. N is usually the ID of the slide record inside, but a part may be named `Slide.iwa` or end in `-2`, and names move between slides when slides are deleted; find slides by their IDs ([0161](../experiments/0161-deleted-slide.md), [0162](../experiments/0162-ten-slides.md)). The theme's images, including slide thumbnails, are stored under `Data/`. *Likely* ([0024](../experiments/0024-keynote-and-numbers.md), [0027](../experiments/0027-document-roots.md)). Each master slide has a 356 × 200 thumbnail named `mt-UUID-N.jpg`, where the UUID is the master's and stays the same in every presentation made from the theme. [release: differs by release] Basic White's masters have one set of UUIDs in Keynote 15.4 and another in 14.5 ([0466](../experiments/0466-keynote-14-5.md)). Slides have `st-UUID-N.jpg`. A master's thumbnail is redrawn only once the master is shown, so a changed master can keep an old thumbnail until then ([0291](../experiments/0291-test-theme.md)).

Rerunning every scripted Keynote experiment with a theme that differs from Basic White only in its images gave the same objects, references, texts and record types. So nothing found here depends on the theme's images, apart from the six type-11014 colours that come with Basic White's photos ([0294](../experiments/0294-resource-colours.md)). *Likely* ([0292](../experiments/0292-test-theme-reruns.md)). Tables made with that theme had one or two more type-4009 lists: the calculation engine's lists for its owners, which the theme brings a different set of ([0293](../experiments/0293-calculation-owners.md), see [numbers.md](numbers.md#the-calculation-engine)). [release: 15.4 and 14.5 differ] Basic White differs a little between releases: in 15.4, 26 empty texts on its template slides use the *Brødtekst* (Body) paragraph style, and in 14.5, 17 of them use an unnamed variation of *Liten infotekst* (Caption Small) at 56 points instead, one more type-2022 style ([0466](../experiments/0466-keynote-14-5.md)).

A theme file (`.kth`) is a Keynote document in the same container, with a different extension. Its name isn't stored in it: Keynote takes the name from the presentation it was saved from. *Likely* ([0291](../experiments/0291-test-theme.md))

## Slides

Show order is the order of field 3.2 alone: moving a slide changes only that list ([0160](../experiments/0160-moved-slide.md)). *Confirmed* by writing it, which also showed that a slide node left out of the list isn't shown ([0378](../experiments/0378-writing-slide-order.md)). A duplicated slide is a full copy with new IDs ([0159](../experiments/0159-duplicated-slide.md)), and a deleted slide leaves nothing behind ([0161](../experiments/0161-deleted-slide.md)). A template slide's field 10 is its layout name in the app's language ([0165](../experiments/0165-slide-layouts.md); *confirmed* by writing it, [0415](../experiments/0415-writing-a-layout-name.md)). An empty title placeholder is an empty text record; its prompt is only in the layout ([0168](../experiments/0168-untouched-title.md)). Line feeds in titles and presenter notes are paragraph breaks ([0163](../experiments/0163-multi-paragraph-notes.md), [0164](../experiments/0164-two-line-title.md)); carriage returns are too, and U+2028 is a line break within a paragraph, as in Pages (*confirmed* by writing them, [0429](../experiments/0429-writing-breaks-in-a-keynote-title.md)). *Likely*

## Slide contents

Shapes added to a slide (text boxes) are type 2011, images type 3005 and tables types 6000 and 6001, as in Pages and Numbers. Each drawable's field 1.2 names the slide it is on. Placeholders from the layout, such as the title, are type 7. Lines and shapes have the same geometry and style identifiers as in Pages ([0166](../experiments/0166-keynote-line.md), [0167](../experiments/0167-keynote-shape-text.md)); a line's rotation and its length, from its path's natural width, are *confirmed* to work as in Pages by writing them ([0439](../experiments/0439-writing-a-keynote-line.md)). *Likely* ([0068](../experiments/0068-keynote-text-box.md), [0069](../experiments/0069-keynote-image.md), [0073](../experiments/0073-keynote-table.md))

A slide's transition is in its field 4.2.8: the effect's name in field 2, its duration in seconds in field 3, the delay before an automatic transition in field 5 (default 0.5) and whether it starts automatically in field 6. Duration and delay are doubles. *Confirmed* by writing them ([0071](../experiments/0071-transition.md), [0094](../experiments/0094-automatic-transition.md), [0302](../experiments/0302-writing-a-transition.md))

Effect names are Keynote's own identifiers: `none`, `apple:dissolve` (AppleScript `dissolve`), `apple:slide` (`move in`), `apple:push` (`push`), `apple:3D-cube` (`cube`) and `apple:magic-move-implied-motion-path` (`magic move`). *Likely* ([0091](../experiments/0091-move-in-transition.md)–[0095](../experiments/0095-magic-move.md)). The identifiers of the other 38 effects AppleScript offers are listed in [0196](../experiments/0196-every-transition.md), *confirmed* by writing each and reading the effect back ([0347](../experiments/0347-every-transition-by-writing.md)). Some use the `apple:` form, and others a class name such as `com.apple.iWork.Keynote.BLTBlinds`. *Likely* [release: 15.4 only] `apple:radial wipe` is new in Keynote 15.4: Keynote 14.5 keeps it through a save but doesn't know it, and its AppleScript can neither set nor report it ([0466](../experiments/0466-keynote-14-5.md), [0467](../experiments/0467-opening-15-4-presentations-in-keynote-14-5.md)).

A build is a type-8 record listed in slide field 2: field 1 names the object (*confirmed* by writing it, [0450](../experiments/0450-writing-a-build-s-object.md)), field 2 the delivery (such as `All at Once`), and field 4.18 the effect, with `In`, `Out` or `Action` in 4.18.1 for a build-in, build-out or action, *confirmed* ([0263](../experiments/0263-build-out.md), [0264](../experiments/0264-action-build.md); and by writing `Out` into a build-in, [0449](../experiments/0449-writing-a-build-direction.md)); an action's settings, such as a rotation's angle in 4.9, sit beside the effect in field 4, an identifier such as `apple:dissolve character` in 4.18.2 and the duration in 4.18.3. Slide field 43 lists type-153 records, probably the build order. *Likely* ([0257](../experiments/0257-build.md)). A build plays only with both its entry in field 2 and a type-153 stage in field 43; without either it is ignored, and a stage listed twice plays once (*confirmed* by writing them, [0448](../experiments/0448-writing-a-build-s-stages.md)). Writing the delivery text doesn't change the number of stages Keynote plays, so the stages probably come from the type-153 records, one per stage ([0368](../experiments/0368-writing-a-build-delivery.md)).

A slide's background is the fill in its slide style (type 9, property 11.1); a colour in 11.1.1 is *confirmed* by writing it ([0346](../experiments/0346-writing-a-slide-background.md)). A dynamic background is an image fill (field 3) showing a pre-rendered still, a `motionBackground-…` data file (the same file whatever the preset's angle, scale and speed, [0292](../experiments/0292-test-theme-reruns.md)), plus field 200 naming a type-26 motion background style. That style holds the generator's name, such as `KNGradientLinearBase`, colour stops as in a gradient fill, and parameters such as the scale (64) and speed (70). *Likely* ([0271](../experiments/0271-dynamic-background.md)). Keynote renders the background from the style's stops, even when exporting, and ignores the still, which is there for readers that can't (*confirmed* by writing them, [0440](../experiments/0440-writing-a-dynamic-background.md)).

Bulleted body text uses the same text records and list styles as Pages; the theme's default body list style is a bullet (`•` in field 16, repeated once per level). *Likely* ([0072](../experiments/0072-keynote-bullets.md)). Field 16 as the bullet Keynote draws is *confirmed* by writing it ([0414](../experiments/0414-writing-a-keynote-bullet.md)).
