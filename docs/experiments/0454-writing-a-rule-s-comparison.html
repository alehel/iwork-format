0454: Writing a rule's comparison#

App release: 15.4

Status: run

A highlighting rule's settings hold its kind in 3.1.1.1, 7 for greater than, and its value in 3.1.1.5.2.2 (0259, 0391); a filter's rule keeps its kind in 7.1.1 the same way (0258). This experiment writes every kind from 0 to 12 and reads which cells Numbers highlights.

Setup#

Numbers15.4, in Norwegian
macOS26.5.2 (25F84)
Date2026-11-18
Inputsamples/0259/highlight.numbers: n 1, 2, 3, 4 in B2:B5, highlighted green by rule 905589, greater than 2 (3.1.1.1 = 7)
Writerrecipes/0302-set-path.py; exported with recipes/0372-in-app.sh and read with recipes/0384-pdf-colours.swift

Recipe#

mkdir -p samples/0454
O=samples/0454
for k in $(seq 0 12); do
  python3 -I recipes/0302-set-path.py samples/0259/highlight.numbers $O/kind-$k.numbers 905589 3.1.1.1 varint $k
  sh recipes/0372-in-app.sh Numbers $O/kind-$k.numbers "export d to POSIX file \"$PWD/$O/kind-$k.pdf\" as PDF"
  swift recipes/0384-pdf-colours.swift $O/kind-$k.pdf 220,130 220,150 220,170 220,190
done

A cell counts as highlighted when its red is below 0.9 at the cell's middle.

Observation#

H marks a highlighted cell, for the values 1, 2, 3 and 4, against the rule's value 2:

Kind1234Reads as
0nothing matches
1, 2, 3, 5Hequal to 2
4, 6HHHnot equal to 2
7HHgreater than 2
8HHHgreater than or equal to 2
9Hless than 2
10HHless than or equal to 2
11, 12HHHHeverything matches

Conclusion#

The kind codes 7 to 10 are the four inequalities: greater than, greater than or equal, less than and less than or equal. Codes 4 and 6 match everything except 2, and 1, 2, 3 and 5 only 2: with one-digit numbers, tests of equality and text tests such as starts with, ends with or contains give the same answer, so these values can't tell those codes apart. Codes 11 and 12 match every cell: they are probably between and not between, which need a second value this rule doesn't have. The codes are their own list, as the pivot's summary codes are (0279).

Confidence#

Confirmed: kinds 7, 8, 9 and 10 as greater than, greater than or equal, less than and less than or equal, by writing them. Likely: 1, 2, 3 and 5 test equality or a text match, 4 and 6 the opposite. Unknown: which is which among those, and 11 and 12.
