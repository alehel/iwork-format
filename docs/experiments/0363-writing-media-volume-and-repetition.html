0363: Writing a sound's volume and repetition#

Status: run

A sound or movie is a type-3007 record whose field 7 is the volume, from 0 to 1, and field 24 the repetition, 0 none and 1 loop (0185). This experiment writes both into a sound and a movie and reads them back through Keynote's AppleScript properties: clip volume and repetition method for an audio clip, and movie volume and repetition method for a movie.

Setup#

Keynote15.4
macOS26.5.2 (25F84)
Date2026-11-10
Inputsamples/0185/audio.key and samples/0185/movie.key: one slide with a sound or a movie, record 2652476, volume 1.0 and repetition 0
Writer[recipes/0302-set-path.py](../recipes/0302-set-path.py), with the writer of 0287

Recipe#

mkdir -p samples/0363
R=recipes/0302-set-path.py
python3 -I $R samples/0185/audio.key samples/0363/quarter-loop.key 2652476 7 float 0.25 24 varint 1
python3 -I $R samples/0185/audio.key samples/0363/back-and-forth.key 2652476 24 varint 2
python3 -I $R samples/0185/movie.key samples/0363/movie-silent.key 2652476 7 float 0 24 varint 2

Keynote opened each file on its own and read the properties of the media on slide 1.

Observation#

FileField 7Field 24VolumeRepetition method
audio.key1.00100none
quarter-loop.key0.25125loop
back-and-forth.key1.02100loop back and forth
movie.key1.00100none
movie-silent.key020loop back and forth

Conclusion#

Field 7 is the volume as a fraction, which AppleScript shows as a percentage. Field 24 is the repetition: 0 none, 1 loop and 2 loop back and forth. The same fields work for a sound and a movie.

Confidence#

Confirmed: field 7 as the volume and field 24 as the repetition, with 2 meaning loop back and forth, by writing them.
