0101: A hidden title#

App release: 15.4

Status: run

0025 found a slide's title in a type-7 placeholder. This experiment hides the title, to see how a hidden placeholder is stored.

Setup#

Keynote15.4 (build M15.4-7051.0.79-2)
macOS26.5.2 (25F84)
Date2026-11-09
BaselineExperiment 0025, empty.key

Recipe#

mkdir -p samples/0101
osascript recipes/0096-keynote-objects.applescript "$PWD/samples/0101/notitle.key" notitle "$PWD/samples/0023/red-8x4.png"

Observation#

The recipe sets slide 1's title showing to false. The slide (type 5) lost its field 7, which named the title placeholder 2652199, and a field 42. The title placeholder no longer appears among the slide's references. A type-7 record with a different ID remains, with a different position.

Conclusion#

A slide's field 7 names its title placeholder. When the title is hidden, the slide stops referring to it, so a reader that follows the slide's fields won't draw a title. That fits the layout's placeholders being optional parts of a slide.

Corrected 2026-11-25: field 7 is a list of what the slide shows, as is field 42. Both lose the title placeholder, 2652199, when the title is hidden, and keep their other entries. Field 5 is what names the title placeholder, and still does; the placeholder's own record is unchanged. keynote.md describes this, which 0312 confirmed by writing.

Evidence#

2021#5 11.1: 0 → 1 says that field 11.1 of the type-2021 record with ID 5 held 0 and holds 1; without an ID, that some record of the type does. = is a value it holds, ≠ one it doesn't, ~ bytes it contains, and ×2 how often a field occurs. These statements were checked against the documents by script (fixtures/evidence.py); only means that nothing else differs but the previews. What the app showed, and what follows from it, is the experiment's own.
