0286: A section background#

Status: run

The Document sidebar's Section tab can give a section's pages a background. Its colour well opens a popover that needs Pages in front. This experiment fills the section with yellow, to find where page backgrounds are kept.

Setup#

Pages15.4 (build M15.4-7051.0.79-2), in Norwegian
macOS26.5.2 (25F84)
Date2026-11-09
Baselinesamples/0236/plain.pages

Recipe#

  1. Make a document with the body text One: ``bash osascript -e 'tell application "Pages" to set body text of (make new document) to "One"'
  2. With Pages in front, click in One, open the Document sidebar's Section tab (Inndeling), click the Background (Bakgrunn) colour well, and choose the yellow in the swatches' top row. The whole page turns yellow.
  3. Save it as samples/0286/yellow-section.pages.

Observation#

The only record that changes, apart from the creation time, is the section (type 10011, 0016). It gains:

30: { 1: { 1: 1, 3: 1.0, 4: 0.943, 5: 0.339, 6: 1.0, 12: 1, 13: 1.0 } }

Conclusion#

Section field 30 is the background of the section's pages, a fill in the layout of shape fills (0077), here a colour in field 1. Since sections hold the background, each section of a document can have its own. A reader should paint each page with its section's field 30 before drawing anything else. The colour layout is the usual one: red, green and blue in 3–5 and alpha in 6.

Confidence#

Likely: section field 30 is the page background, as a fill.
