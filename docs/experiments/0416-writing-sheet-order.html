0416: Writing the order of sheets#

App release: 15.4

Status: run

A Numbers document root (object 1) lists its sheets in order in field 1 (0037). Keynote's show order is a similar list, confirmed by writing it (0378). This experiment reorders the sheets and reads them through AppleScript.

Setup#

Numbers15.4, in Norwegian
macOS26.5.2 (25F84)
Date2026-11-18
Inputsamples/0037/two-sheets.numbers: the sheets Ark 1 and Second, the second active, listed in that order in field 1 of object 1
WriterThe new [recipes/0416-reorder.py](../recipes/0416-reorder.py); read back with [recipes/0372-in-app.sh](../recipes/0372-in-app.sh)

Recipe#

mkdir -p samples/0416
X=recipes/0416-reorder.py
F=samples/0037/two-sheets.numbers
O=samples/0416
cp $F $O/original.numbers
python3 -I $X $F $O/reversed.numbers 1 1 2,1
python3 -I $X $F $O/second-only.numbers 1 1 2
for f in original reversed second-only; do
  sh recipes/0372-in-app.sh Numbers $O/$f.numbers 'set out to {}
repeat with s in sheets of d
  set end of out to name of s
end repeat
return {out, name of active sheet of d}'
done

Observation#

FileField 1 writtenSheetsActive
original.numbersArk 1, SecondSecond
reversed.numbersreversedSecond, Ark 1Second
second-only.numbersSecond onlySecondSecond

Conclusion#

The order of field 1 is the order of the sheets, and a sheet left out of it isn't part of the document, though its records are still in the file, just as with Keynote's slides. The active sheet is kept by identity, not position: Second stayed active when it moved first.

Confidence#

Confirmed: field 1 of a Numbers document root as its sheets in order, by writing it.
