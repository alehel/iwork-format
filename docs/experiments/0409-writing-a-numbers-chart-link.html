0409: Writing a Numbers chart's link to its cells#

App release: 15.4

Status: run

A Numbers chart keeps a copy of its data in 10000.7, like charts in Pages and Keynote, and also links to its table: 10000.8 names a type-12006 record whose field 3 holds cell formulas for the series values (3.1), category labels (3.3) and series names (3.4) (0225). This experiment writes the copy, a cell, and the link, and compares the exported sheet with one where Numbers made the same change itself.

Setup#

Numbers15.4, in Norwegian
macOS26.5.2 (25F84)
Date2026-11-18
Inputsamples/0225/linked.numbers: x = 1, 3 and y = 2, 4 in B2:C3, charted as two series over r1 and r2. Chart 905458 has the values in 10000.7.3; its link, record 905524, has in 3.1 a first formula made of a reference to B2, a reference to B3 and node 29
Writerrecipes/0383-set-nth-path.py and recipes/0342-cell-number.py; exported with recipes/0372-in-app.sh and compared with recipes/0399-pdf-diff.swift, which can now compare part of a page

Recipe#

mkdir -p samples/0409
N=recipes/0383-set-nth-path.py
F=samples/0225/linked.numbers
O=samples/0409
cp $F $O/original.numbers
python3 -I $N $F $O/cache-9.numbers 905458 10000.7.3:1.1:1.1 double 9
python3 -I recipes/0342-cell-number.py $F $O/cell-b2-9.numbers 1 1 9
python3 -I $N $F $O/link-to-c2.numbers 905524 3.1:1.1.1:1.26.1 varint 4
python3 -I $N $F $O/link-to-column-c.numbers 905524 3.1:1.1.1:1.26.1 varint 4 3.1:1.1.1:2.26.1 varint 4
for f in original cache-9 cell-b2-9 link-to-c2 link-to-column-c; do
  sh recipes/0372-in-app.sh Numbers $O/$f.numbers "export d to POSIX file \"$PWD/$O/$f.pdf\" as PDF"
done

As references, Numbers itself set B2 to 9 in one copy (numbers-b2-9), and B2 to 2 and B3 to 4 in another (numbers-x-is-y), through AppleScript, before exporting. Column 4 is index 2, column C, in the zigzag form of 0371.

Conclusion#

Numbers draws a chart from its link to the table, worked out when it opens the document, and ignores the copy of the data in 10000.7: a written copy changed nothing, and a written cell redrew the chart exactly as Numbers does when the cell is edited. The series formulas are ranges: a reference to the first cell and one to the last, joined by node 29. Moving only the first end to C2 made the range B2:C3, read across each row (1, 2, 3, 4), and moving both ends to column C made series x the same as y. A reader of a Numbers chart should evaluate its link formulas against the table and use 10000.7 only where there is no link, as in Pages and Keynote.

Evidence#

2021#5 11.1: 0 → 1 says that field 11.1 of the type-2021 record with ID 5 held 0 and holds 1; without an ID, that some record of the type does. = is a value it holds, ≠ one it doesn't, ~ bytes it contains, and ×2 how often a field occurs. These statements were checked against the documents by script (fixtures/evidence.py); only means that nothing else differs but the previews. Confidence#

Confirmed: a Numbers chart's values come from its type-12006 link formulas, not its copy in 10000.7, and node 29 joins two references into a range, by writing them.
