0470: Opening the 15.4 samples in 16.0#

App release: 16.0

Status: run

The 16.0 apps write the same format as 15.4 (0469). This experiment opens every sample the 15.4 apps saved or the spec's writer wrote, in all three apps, and checks what 16.0 reads, as 0462 and 0467 did for 14.5.

Setup#

AppsPages, Numbers and Keynote 16.0 (build M16.0-7052.0.68-2), in Norwegian, the only copies installed
macOS26.5.2 (25F84)
Date2026-11-24
InputEvery .pages and .numbers under samples/0001 to samples/0460, and every .key under samples/NNNN/ and samples/theme/NNNN/, leaving out what 0462 and 0467 left out: the files written to make the apps fail (0379, 0394, 0396, 0424, 0434 and 0451) and the samples later releases saved (0461 on). Also left out: experiments 0179 and 0180, whose seven password-protected documents would hold up a run nobody watches with their password prompt (with them, 0179's two unprotected documents). 1,211 documents
Scripts[recipes/0462-open-in-app.py](../recipes/0462-open-in-app.py) and [recipes/0467-open-keynote.py](../recipes/0467-open-keynote.py), unchanged: Pages' body text against the body's text record, Numbers' sheets and first table, and Keynote's slide count, titles and notes against the file

Recipe#

for r in "1 178" "181 393" "395 395" "397 423" "425 433" "435 450" "452 460"; do
  python3 -I recipes/0462-open-in-app.py samples $r
done
python3 -I recipes/0467-open-keynote.py samples 0379,0180,0466,0467,0468,0469
python3 -I recipes/0467-open-keynote.py samples/theme 0379,0180
for n in 37 38; do
  sh recipes/0372-in-app.sh Keynote samples/0196/all.key "set p to transition properties of slide $n of d
return transition effect of p"
done
python3 -I recipes/0465-saved-by.py samples

Observation#

ResultDocuments
Pages and Numbers: opened, and the body text agrees with the file (Pages) or the sheets and table were read (Numbers)887
Pages: opened, and the body text differs from the file1 (0419/no-body.pages)
Keynote: opened; slide count, titles and notes agree with the file323 (244 under samples/NNNN/, 79 under samples/theme/), 484 slides
Not opened0

No alert was shown. Every reading went through "Keynote Creator Studio" and so on, 16.0.

0419/no-body.pages has its body switch written off over the body's text hello. 16.0 reports no body text for it, as 15.4 did when it was made (0419) and as 14.5 did (0462): the script compares with the file, not with 15.4.

16.0 reports the transition of 0196's slide 37 as fade and move and of slide 38 as radial wipe, which 14.5 couldn't report (0467).

Afterwards, every sample's build history still names the release its experiment names, apart from 0467/resaved.key, which 14.5 saved over 15.4 on purpose in 0467. Opening and closing unsaved left the files as they were.

0467 gave 486 slides for the same 323 presentations, but each presentation's count agreed with the slide nodes its file lists, in that run and in this one, so the totals should be the same. 347 and 137 slides in the two folders add up to 484. The earlier figure can't be checked again, since 14.5 isn't installed.

Conclusion#

The 16.0 apps open every document the 15.4 apps saved in these experiments, and every one written from this spec, and read the same text, tables, slides, titles and notes from them as 15.4 did. Unlike 14.5, Keynote 16.0 knows the radial wipe. Whether 16.0 also draws the samples the same is the next experiment's question.

Confidence#

Confirmed: the 16.0 apps open 1,211 documents from 15.4 and from the spec's writer, and read what 15.4 read. Unknown: whether they open the password-protected samples, which this run left out.
