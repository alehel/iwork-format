0469: New documents in the 16.0 apps#

App release: 16.0

Status: run

Pages, Numbers and Keynote updated themselves to 16.0, replacing 14.5. 15.4 isn't installed any more either, so the 16.0 apps can be compared only with the documents 15.4 left. This experiment reruns, unchanged, every recipe that 0461, 0464 and 0466 reran in 14.5, and compares each 16.0 document with the one 15.4 made from the same recipe.

Setup#

AppsPages, Numbers and Keynote 16.0 (build M16.0-7052.0.68-2, bundle version 7052.0.68), in Norwegian, installed as "Pages Creator Studio" and so on in /Applications; Spotlight lists no other copy
macOS26.5.2 (25F84)
Date2026-11-24
RecipesPages and Numbers: 0001, 0002 and 0026 (0461), and the 24 of 0464. Keynote: the 14 of 0466, with Basic White and IWORK_KEYNOTE_THEME unset. 43 documents in all
Compared withEach experiment's own 15.4 sample, with [recipes/0461-compare-structure.py](../recipes/0461-compare-structure.py)
ScriptThe new [recipes/0469-rerun-in-16-0.sh](../recipes/0469-rerun-in-16-0.sh), which runs the recipes and the comparisons

Recipe#

bash recipes/0469-rerun-in-16-0.sh samples/0469
for i in 2 3; do
  osascript recipes/0044-numbers-merge.applescript "$PWD/samples/0469/0044-merged-run$i.numbers"
  osascript recipes/0047-numbers-second-table.applescript "$PWD/samples/0469/0047-two-tables-run$i.numbers"
  osascript recipes/0078-numbers-error.applescript "$PWD/samples/0469/0078-error-run$i.numbers"
done

The second step makes three of the Numbers documents twice more, to see whether their calculation engines differ from run to run (see below).

Observation#

Every recipe ran unchanged, and the 16.0 apps saved every document with no alert. Keynote 0196 ran whole, radial wipe included, and its slides carry the same transition identifiers as the 15.4 presentation's, apple:radial wipe among them.

In all 43 pairs: the same parts, the same version in header field 2.2 for every record type both releases write, and the same top-level fields for every type but four. The differences, besides UUIDs:

What 16.0 shares with 15.4 and not with 14.5: the empty type-4011 record in CalculationEngine.iwa, named by field 15 of the engine's root (type 4000); field 32, 0, on every slide node (type 4), 19 of them in 0159-dup.key; blankMoviePosterImage kept as an app resource rather than in the package; and Basic White's paragraph styles, 225 of them, with no unnamed Liten infotekst variation.

Three Numbers pairs (0044, 0047 and 0078) had one owner (type 4008) more or fewer in the calculation engine, and in 0047 three more type-4009 lists and no engine UUID (field 17 of type 4000). Made twice more in 16.0, those documents had 13, 25 and 13 owners, and 0047 its UUID back, as 15.4 had for 0044 and 0047, while 15.4's 0078 and 14.5's 0044 and 0078 have 14. The counts follow the run, not the release. Field 17 is absent from 20 of 22 Numbers documents from 15.4 under samples/0026 to samples/0079 too.

Slide thumbnails (st-UUID-N.jpg) differ in number in some pairs, as in 0466.

Conclusion#

The 16.0 apps write the same format as 15.4. Everything these recipes make, from text, shapes and tables to formulas, charts, slides, notes, transitions and placeholders, is laid out the same way, down to the record versions. Where 14.5 and 15.4 differ, 16.0 sides with 15.4, apart from Numbers' Table Title 1 style, which it leaves out as 14.5 does.

What is new in 16.0 is all in ViewState.iwa, which records how the document was last shown and which a reader can ignore: one more field in each app's view-state record. What they hold is unknown.

16.0 writes the file format version 27.0.1. Its build history names its template (dev/winter2026) rather than a release number.

Whether 16.0 opens and draws the 15.4 samples as 15.4 did is the next experiment's question. Whether 15.4 or 14.5 open a 16.0 document can't be tested on this Mac now: the 14.5 apps are kept only as a disk image, and 15.4 not at all.

Confidence#

Confirmed: for the 43 documents of these recipes, the 16.0 apps write the same structure as 15.4, apart from the differences listed. Unknown: what the new view-state fields are for.
