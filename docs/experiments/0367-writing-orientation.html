0367: Writing the orientation#

App release: 15.4

Status: run

In the document root, field 42 is the orientation (0 portrait, 1 landscape), and in a landscape document the paper width and height (fields 30 and 31) are already swapped (0155). This experiment checks which of the two decides the page Pages draws. It writes the flag alone, the swapped size alone, and both.

Setup#

Pages15.4
macOS26.5.2 (25F84)
Date2026-11-10
Inputsamples/0002/hello.pages: portrait A4, 595.28 × 841.89, field 42 = 0
Writerrecipes/0302-set-path.py, with the writer of 0287

Recipe#

mkdir -p samples/0367
R=recipes/0302-set-path.py
B=samples/0002/hello.pages
python3 -I $R $B samples/0367/flag-only.pages 1 42 varint 1
python3 -I $R $B samples/0367/swap-only.pages 1 30 float 841.89 31 float 595.28
python3 -I $R samples/0367/swap-only.pages samples/0367/both.pages 1 42 varint 1

Pages opened each file on its own, exported a PDF, and saved a copy (-resaved), whose fields were read back.

Observation#

FileWritten: 30 × 31, 42PDF pageAfter Pages saved: 30 × 31, 42
flag-only.pages595.28 × 841.89, 1596 × 842595.28 × 841.89, 1
swap-only.pages841.89 × 595.28, 0842 × 596841.89 × 595.28, 0
both.pages841.89 × 595.28, 1842 × 596841.89 × 595.28, 1

In each PDF the text starts at the margins, 57 points from the left and 58 from the top.

Conclusion#

The page is drawn from fields 30 and 31 alone. Field 42 doesn't turn the page, and Pages neither corrects it nor the size to match: it saves both as they were written. To make a landscape page, write the swapped size and set 42 = 1, as Pages itself does, so its orientation control agrees.

Evidence#

2021#5 11.1: 0 → 1 says that field 11.1 of the type-2021 record with ID 5 held 0 and holds 1; without an ID, that some record of the type does. These statements were checked against the documents by script (fixtures/evidence.py); only means that nothing else differs but the previews. What the app showed, and what follows from it, is the experiment's own.

Confidence#

Confirmed: the page size comes from fields 30 and 31, not field 42, by writing them. Likely: field 42 only drives the orientation shown in the Document sidebar, which wasn't looked at.
