0434: Leaving out package files#

App release: 15.4

Status: run

Besides its Index/ parts, a document's ZIP holds Metadata/Properties.plist, Metadata/DocumentIdentifier, Metadata/BuildVersionHistory.plist and three previews (container.md). The writer of 0287 already left out the previews. This experiment leaves out each of the others, and the view state, and asks whether Pages opens the document.

Setup#

Pages15.4, in Norwegian
macOS26.5.2 (25F84)
Date2026-11-18
Inputsamples/0002/hello.pages: seven parts under Index/, the three files under Metadata/, and preview.jpg, preview-micro.jpg and preview-web.jpg
WriterThe new [recipes/0434-drop-entries.py](../recipes/0434-drop-entries.py); opened with [recipes/0372-in-app.sh](../recipes/0372-in-app.sh)

Recipe#

mkdir -p samples/0434
X=recipes/0434-drop-entries.py
F=samples/0002/hello.pages
O=samples/0434
python3 -I $X $F $O/no-identifier.pages Metadata/DocumentIdentifier
python3 -I $X $F $O/no-properties.pages Metadata/Properties.plist
python3 -I $X $F $O/no-history.pages Metadata/BuildVersionHistory.plist
python3 -I $X $F $O/no-previews.pages 'preview*'
python3 -I $X $F $O/no-metadata-folder.pages 'Metadata/*'
python3 -I $X $F $O/only-index.pages 'Metadata/*' 'preview*'
python3 -I $X $F $O/no-viewstate.pages Index/ViewState.iwa
for f in no-identifier no-properties no-history no-previews no-metadata-folder only-index no-viewstate; do
  sh recipes/0372-in-app.sh Pages $O/$f.pages 'return {body text of d, font of word 1 of body text of d}'
done

Observation#

FileLeft outPages
no-identifier.pagesDocumentIdentifieropens: hello, HelveticaNeue
no-properties.pagesProperties.plistopens
no-history.pagesBuildVersionHistory.plistopens
no-previews.pagesthe three previewsopens
no-metadata-folder.pagesall of Metadata/opens
only-index.pagesMetadata/ and the previewsdoesn't open, with no alert
no-viewstate.pagesIndex/ViewState.iwadoesn't open, with no alert

After either of the last two, Pages opened no other document either, not even ones it had just opened, until it was quit and started again. Each was tried twice, with the same result.

Conclusion#

Pages doesn't need any single file outside Index/: each of the three Metadata/ files, and the previews, can be left out, and so can all of Metadata/ at once. But a ZIP with nothing outside Index/ makes Pages stop opening documents altogether, and so does a document without its view state part, which Metadata.iwa lists as a component (0394). So a writer should always include ViewState.iwa and keep at least one entry besides Index/, such as the Metadata/ files Pages writes. Why the combination matters is unknown.

Confidence#

Confirmed: Pages opens a document without any one of the Metadata/ files or the previews, and without all of Metadata/, but not without its view state, nor with only Index/, by writing them. Why is unknown.
