3. Styles#

How text is formatted: character, paragraph and list styles, the properties they set, and how they combine. The text refers to them through its run tables (2. Text). Every finding links to the experiments that support it, with a confidence level as defined in CONTRIBUTING.md. Records, object IDs and types are as described in 1. Container.

Styles made by formatting#

Changing the size of part of the text, or making it bold or italic, creates a new, unnamed character style (type 2021) in DocumentStylesheet.iwa. It inherits (field 1.3) from a character style whose identifier is character-style-null. Confirmed (0004, 0007, 0008)

Formatting a whole paragraph instead creates an unnamed paragraph style (type 2022) that inherits from the paragraph's style and carries the change. Likely (0008, 0149)

Several properties set on one range go into one style (0152). Setting a property to the value it already has makes no style (0153), and styles nothing uses are dropped on save (0154). A template's styles are copied into the document with their own values, such as white text in the black template (0157). Likely

Character properties#

Field 11 of a character style or a paragraph style holds the character properties it sets. A property that is left out is inherited, and one set to 0 is turned off (confirmed by writing both into a character style over a paragraph style, 0392). Likely (0004, 0007, 0008)

Field 11.nPropertyEncoding
1Boldvarint: 1 bold, 0 not bold
2Italicvarint: 1 italic
3Size in pointsfloat
5Font name, such as HelveticaNeuetext
7Text colourmessage: red, green and blue in fields 3, 4 and 5 and alpha in field 6, as floats from 0 to 1, in the colour space named by field 12: 1 (or absent) sRGB, 2 Display P3 (0329); field 1 = 1 is RGB. The apps write 1, and five such colours matched sRGB, through AppleScript and on the drawn page (0328); AppleScript reports colours converted to Generic RGB (0309). In cell text styles, at least, the colour drawn is 46.1 and this field a copy that the app rewrites from it on saving (0310)
46Text fillmessage: field 1 is the text colour as in 7; this is the one drawn, and the app keeps 7 in step with it when saving (0310). With 1, 2, 3 and 5, confirmed by writing a paragraph style in Pages (0311)
10Baseline positionvarint: 1 superscript, 2 subscript (0203, 0204) Confirmed by writing and measuring (0322).
11Underlinevarint: 1 underlined, 0 not
12Strikethroughvarint: 1 struck through, 0 not
13Capitalisationvarint: 1 all caps, 2 small caps; the stored text keeps its case (0205, 0206) Confirmed by writing and measuring (0322).
14Baseline shiftfloat, in points, up from the baseline (0209) Confirmed by writing and measuring (0322).
16Ligaturesvarint: 0 none; absent means the default (0208)
27Letter spacingfloat, as a fraction of the font size: 0.01 is 1% (0207) Confirmed by writing and measuring (0322).

Field 11.5 is confirmed (0010, 0053), and so are 11.7 (0013), 11.11 and 11.12 (0012); the rest as above.

Paragraph properties#

A paragraph style also has a field 12 for paragraph properties. Likely (0008, 0010)

Field 12.nPropertyEncoding
1Alignmentvarint: 0 left, 1 right, 2 centred, 3 justified, 4 probably natural (the Body style's value, shown as left); 1 and 2 confirmed by writing them and measuring the page (0319)
7First-line indentfloat, in points from the margin, for the first line only (0253; confirmed by writing and measuring, 0320)
11Left indentfloat, in points from the margin, for every line but the first, independent of 7 (0253; confirmed, 0320)
13Line spacingmessage: field 1 is the kind (absent for a multiple of lines; 1 at least, 2 exactly and 4 between, in points, 0281) and field 2 the amount, such as 2.0 for double (0252, 0276) Confirmed by writing and measuring (0321).
19Right indentfloat, in points from the right margin (0253; confirmed, 0320)
20Space after the paragraphfloat, in points (0256) Confirmed by writing and measuring (0321).
21Space before the paragraphfloat, in points (0256) Confirmed by writing and measuring (0321).
25Tab stopsmessage: one 25.1 per stop, with its position in points from the left margin in field 1 (confirmed with left and right stops by writing and measuring, 0325; without stops, Pages uses one every 36 points), its alignment in field 2 (absent for left, 1 centre, 2 right, 3 decimal, 0282) and its leader character in field 3 (a space draws nothing) (0255, 0277)
32Bordermessage: a stroke in the layout of a shape's (colour, width, pattern); property 45 gives its edges as bits (1 top, 2 bottom, 15 all), and property 15 an older single value (1 top, 2 bottom, 4 all). Pages draws from 15; changing 45 alone changes nothing drawn (confirmed by writing and measuring, 0336) (0262, 0273)

Values 0 to 3 are confirmed (0010, 0011); 4 as natural alignment is likely.

Named and unnamed styles#

Named styles such as Body set every property; unnamed ones set only what differs from their parent (field 1.3). Likely (0010). That an unnamed style takes what it leaves out from the style in its field 1.3 is confirmed by writing another parent there (0393).

Field 10 has equalled the number of properties set in every unnamed style so far, and is 57 in the named paragraph styles, which set 59. It doesn't count edits. Unknown (0010, 0011, 0152)

List styles#

A list style (type 2023) stores most properties as nine repeated values, one per list level. Field 11 is the kind of marker (0 none, 2 a text character, 3 a number), field 16 the marker text for kind 2, and field 13 an indent in points that grows by a fixed step per level, moving the marker and text together. Likely (0014); fields 11, 13 and 16 confirmed by writing them and measuring (0334)

Finding a character's formatting#

To find a character's formatting, take each property from the first style that sets it. Look first in its character style (from the field-8 runs), then that style's parent (field 1.3), and so on. Then look in its paragraph style (from the field-5 runs) and that style's parents. A property set to 0 counts as set. A font name in 11.5 may already include a weight, such as HelveticaNeue-Bold; Pages takes only the family from it, and the face from the bold and italic flags (confirmed by writing them: HelveticaNeue-Bold with bold 0 is drawn regular, 0428). Likely for bold, italic, size and font: the rule predicted Pages' own reading of all 19 words of 8 documents (0033, 0034). It also holds for text colour (0042).
