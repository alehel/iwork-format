Keynote#

What is specific to Keynote documents. The container is the same as Pages' (container.md), and text is stored as in text.md. Every finding links to the experiments that support it, with a confidence level as defined in CONTRIBUTING.md.

Observed with Keynote 15.4 on macOS 26.5.2.

Parts#

A new presentation has the seven .iwa parts that Pages documents have, without numbers in their names, plus one Index/Slide-N.iwa per slide and one Index/TemplateSlide-N.iwa per template slide of the theme. N is usually the ID of the slide record inside, but a part may be named Slide.iwa or end in -2, and names move between slides when slides are deleted; find slides by their IDs (0161, 0162). The theme's images, including slide thumbnails, are stored under Data/. Likely (0024, 0027). Each master slide has a 356 × 200 thumbnail named mt-UUID-N.jpg, where the UUID is the master's and stays the same in every presentation made from the theme; slides have st-UUID-N.jpg. A master's thumbnail is redrawn only once the master is shown, so a changed master can keep an old thumbnail until then (0291).

Rerunning every scripted Keynote experiment with a theme that differs from Basic White only in its images gave the same objects, references, texts and record types. So nothing found here depends on the theme's images, apart from the six type-11014 colours that come with Basic White's photos (0294). Likely (0292). Tables made with that theme had one or two more type-4009 lists: the calculation engine's lists for its owners, which the theme brings a different set of (0293, see numbers.md).

A theme file (.kth) is a Keynote document in the same container, with a different extension. Its name isn't stored in it: Keynote takes the name from the presentation it was saved from. Likely (0291)

Slides#

Show order is the order of field 3.2 alone: moving a slide changes only that list (0160). A duplicated slide is a full copy with new IDs (0159), and a deleted slide leaves nothing behind (0161). A template slide's field 10 is its layout name in the app's language (0165). An empty title placeholder is an empty text record; its prompt is only in the layout (0168). Line feeds in titles and presenter notes are paragraph breaks (0163, 0164). Likely

Slide contents#

Shapes added to a slide (text boxes) are type 2011, images type 3005 and tables types 6000 and 6001, as in Pages and Numbers. Each drawable's field 1.2 names the slide it is on. Placeholders from the layout, such as the title, are type 7. Lines and shapes have the same geometry and style identifiers as in Pages (0166, 0167). Likely (0068, 0069, 0073)

A slide's transition is in its field 4.2.8: the effect's name in field 2, its duration in seconds in field 3, the delay before an automatic transition in field 5 (default 0.5) and whether it starts automatically in field 6. Duration and delay are doubles. Confirmed by writing them (0071, 0094, 0302)

Effect names are Keynote's own identifiers: none, apple:dissolve (AppleScript dissolve), apple:slide (move in), apple:push (push), apple:3D-cube (cube) and apple:magic-move-implied-motion-path (magic move). Likely (0091–0095). The identifiers of the other 38 effects AppleScript offers are listed in 0196, confirmed by writing each and reading the effect back (0347). Some use the apple: form, and others a class name such as com.apple.iWork.Keynote.BLTBlinds. Likely

Sounds and movies are type 3007. Field 14 names the media file by data ID, field 15 a poster image for a movie, field 7 the volume (0–1) and field 24 the repetition (0 none, 1 loop, 2 loop back and forth); both are confirmed by writing them into a sound and a movie (0363). Field 23 is 1 when the media has pictures and field 9 is 1 when it has only sound; fields 20 and 21 are a movie's natural size. Field 29 describes the first track: its kind (soun or vide) and time scale. A sound's drawable has a size of 0 × 0. Likely (0185–0187). Pages and Numbers store media in the same record: that sounds and movies are type 3007 with their file in field 14 is confirmed (0188).

A build is a type-8 record listed in slide field 2: field 1 names the object, field 2 the delivery (such as All at Once), and field 4.18 the effect, with In, Out or Action in 4.18.1 for a build-in, build-out or action, confirmed (0263, 0264); an action's settings, such as a rotation's angle in 4.9, sit beside the effect in field 4, an identifier such as apple:dissolve character in 4.18.2 and the duration in 4.18.3. Slide field 43 lists type-153 records, probably the build order. Likely (0257). Writing the delivery text doesn't change the number of stages Keynote plays, so the stages probably come from the type-153 records, one per stage (0368).

A slide's background is the fill in its slide style (type 9, property 11.1); a colour in 11.1.1 is confirmed by writing it (0346). A dynamic background is an image fill (field 3) showing a pre-rendered still, a motionBackground-… data file (the same file whatever the preset's angle, scale and speed, 0292), plus field 200 naming a type-26 motion background style. That style holds the generator's name, such as KNGradientLinearBase, colour stops as in a gradient fill, and parameters such as the scale (64) and speed (70). Likely (0271)

Bulleted body text uses the same text records and list styles as Pages; the theme's default body list style is a bullet (• in field 16). Likely (0072)

Charts#

A chart is a drawable of type 5021 on the slide. Its field 10000.7 holds its data: row names in 7.1, column names in 7.2, and one 7.3 per row listing its values as doubles. Confirmed: Pages stores charts the same way (0067, 0222). A Numbers chart also links to its table: field 10000.8 names a type-12006 record whose field 3 holds formulas, in the cell-formula node form, for the series values (3.1), category labels (3.3) and series names (3.4); 10000.7 keeps a copy of the values. Likely (0225)

A chart also names a chart style (type 5022) in field 10000.9 and a type-5023 record of settings in 10000.10. In the settings, 10000.21 = 1 shows the chart's title and 10000.20 = 1 its legend; turning one off leaves 0. Confirmed by writing them (0298). The border is chart style property 10000.18 = 1, which Keynote sets through an unnamed style whose parent is the theme's chart style, as with text styles. A chart's caption is drawable field 1.11, as for other objects. Likely (0295)

Each part of a chart has such a pair of a style and a settings record, named in field 10000: 10000.9 and 10000.10 the chart (types 5022, 5023), 10000.11 and 10000.12 types 5024 and 5025, 10000.13 and 10000.14 the value axes (types 5026, 5027, two each, the main value axis first), and 10000.15 and 10000.16 the category axis (0297). Field 10000.17 names six series styles (type 5028, chart-0-series_0 and on) and 10000.20 the chart's 25 paragraph styles (type 2022). Shareable properties go in a style, which Keynote overrides through an unnamed child style; a chart's own switches go in its settings record. For the value axis, style property 10000.25 = 1 draws the axis line and 10000.31 = 0 hides the minimum value, and setting 10000.14 = 1 shows the axis name; the category axis shows its name with setting 10000.13 = 1. Likely (0296, 0297)

Writing the grid and the type changes the chart Keynote draws, confirmed in 0317. Field 10000.1 is the chart type and 10000.5 the grouping into series (1 by row, 2 by column, confirmed by writing it, 0350); the data grid in 10000.7 is the same whatever the type or grouping. Likely (0079–0086)
